☁️ Offline-First Architecture

Works at 35,000 feet without WiFi

How PocketTrack ensures you never lose a single rupee of data, even on subways or flights.

1. Instant Local Write

Every transaction is written to your browser's sandboxed IndexedDB storage in under 5 milliseconds with zero latency.

2. Pending Queue Guard

If you're offline, changes enter a persistent sync queue protected against page refreshes or device restarts.

3. Zero-Conflict Cloud Merge

The instant an internet connection returns, changes batch-sync to Google Firebase Firestore with automatic timestamp resolution.
