Account & Data Deletion Request

PocketTrack provides a self-serve, permanent account deletion option directly within the app, fully compliant with the DPDP Act 2023.

How to delete your account:
1. Open the PocketTrack app.
2. Navigate to the Settings (Profile) tab.
3. Scroll down and tap "Support & Account Settings".
4. Tap "Permanently Delete Account & Data".
5. Confirm the action. Your cloud data, local data, and authentication credentials will be immediately expunged.

If you no longer have the app installed, you can launch the Web App below and sign in to access the deletion option. Alternatively, you can submit an account deletion request directly via email.
