Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go! If you wish to delete your PocketTrack account and purge all associated data, you can do so directly from within the app.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent. All your transactions, wallets, budgets, and cloud data will be immediately and permanently purged from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

How to delete your account:

  1. Open the PocketTrack app or web app.
  2. Log into the account you wish to delete.
  3. Open the Power Menu / Command Hub (⚡).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click on Delete Account (Purge Data).
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt.

Alternatively, click the button below to open the web app and sign in to perform the deletion.
