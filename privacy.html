Data Protection & Regulatory Framework (India DPDP Act 2023)

This Privacy Policy explains how PocketTrack Technologies ("Data Fiduciary", "we", "us", or "our") processes, protects, and respects the digital personal data of users ("Data Principals", "you") in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable global data privacy regulations.

1. Identity of the Data Fiduciary

The entity determining the purpose and means of processing personal data under this policy is:
PocketTrack Technologies

2. Personal Data We Collect & Purpose Limitation

In accordance with the principle of Purpose Limitation, PocketTrack only collects data necessary to deliver personal expense tracking services:

  • Account Identifiers: Email address and unique Firebase user UID for authentication and multi-device synchronization.
  • Transaction Data: Amounts, dates, user-created notes, categories (e.g. food, rent, shopping), and wallet tags (Cash, Bank, Card) manually entered or parsed locally via speech/UPI helpers.
  • What We NEVER Collect: We never request, access, or store your bank account passwords, netbanking credentials, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or OTPs. Voice recognition is provided by your browser's built-in speech recognition service. Processing location may vary by browser and device.

3. Data Storage & Cross-Border Transfers

Cloud synchronization is provided via Google Cloud Platform (Firebase Firestore). Data is stored in secure, enterprise-grade cloud datacenters with ISO 27001, SOC 2, and SOC 3 certifications. Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. Cross-border processing by Google infrastructure adheres to the cross-border transfer standards mandated under Section 16 of the DPDP Act 2023.

Third-Party Services

PocketTrack uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) - for user sign-in and account management
  • Cloud Firestore (Google) - for cloud data storage and sync
  • Google Sign-In - for Google account authentication
  • Google Fonts - for typography (loaded from Google servers)
  • jsPDF (CDN) - for PDF report generation
  • Tesseract.js (CDN) - for receipt OCR processing

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not share your financial data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Retention Period & Erasure

Your transaction data is retained only for as long as your account remains active. You may delete individual entries at any time. When you trigger the "Delete Account & Purge All Data" feature in the application, all database records, collections, and local storage keys associated with your account are immediately expunged from production systems.

5. Rights of the Data Principal (Under DPDP Act 2023)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following enforceable statutory rights:

  • Right to Access Information: Obtain a summary of the personal data being processed and data sharing details.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Rectify inaccurate personal data or request permanent deletion of your entire account.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Have your complaints addressed within statutory timelines.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

6. User Protection & Eligibility

PocketTrack is designed for users aged 18 and above. We never collect sensitive personal information, banking credentials, biometrics, government identifiers, or location telemetry from any user. All expense logs and financial entries are sandboxed and encrypted under the user's isolated account.
