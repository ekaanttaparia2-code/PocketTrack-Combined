Frequently Asked Questions

Is PocketTrack really free to use?+
Yes! The Free Forever tier gives you unlimited income and expense logging, 100 voice speech entries, 5 ledger contacts, and 2 shared Splitwise spaces. For heavy power users, our Starter plan begins at just ₹50/month, and Pro Plus at ₹100/month or a one-time lifetime unlock!
How does the Smart Context Engine know who I paid?+
When you type or speak a phrase like "Paid 500 to Rahul", our local NLP parser searches your active Ledger and Spaces contacts, calculates whether it's a debt or reimbursement, and prompts a 1-tap connection.
Can I install PocketTrack on my phone?+
Yes! PocketTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android, open in Chrome and tap "Install App". On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, and choose "Add to Home Screen".
